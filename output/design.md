# Design: How the Apple and NEXT references shaped Campus Customs

**Goal:** keep a shopper on the site from the first screen to a product page, in Yale colors.
**Base palette:** Yale Blue `#00356B` (header strip, buttons, banners), light grey canvas `#F5F5F7`, white cards, and one gold accent `#B07D0C` for "Family Gifts", themes and badges.

## Borrowed from NEXT

| What NEXT does | What we built | How it keeps customers on the site |
|---|---|---|
| Search left, logo center, account + checkout right, uppercase link row | Sticky 3-tier header: search · Campus Customs crest · Log In / **Create Account**, then HOME · PRODUCTS · HOODIES · SWEATSHIRTS · TEES · OUTERWEAR · **FAMILY GIFTS** · ABOUT US | Search and every category are one click away on every page, so nobody has to go "back to home" to keep shopping. |
| Edge-to-edge split hero with a white label tag and "FEATURING / NAME" | Two panels: *Game Day Edit / The Vintage Bulldog* on Yale blue, *Family Pride / Yale Mom, Dad & Grandpa* on light grey | The first screen shows two products and two kinds of buyer (students, families). Both panels are links, so the first click lands on products. |
| Centred serif "Style & Inspiration" carousel with TRENDING labels | **"Find Your Yale"** carousel by theme (Family, Colleges, Schools, Sports, Classic) with a scroll progress bar | It invites browsing by identity, and the progress bar shows there's more to see. |
| Square image tiles with names grouped as FEATURED / SPORTS | Tile grids for **every Residential College** and **every sport** | It gives shoppers a page that's "theirs" (Saybrook, Hockey), a strong reason to stay and buy. |

## What this did for keeping customers on the site
1. **More ways into the catalogue.** The home page has five: search, category links, sub-type icons, a theme carousel, and college/sport tiles. Whatever a shopper has in mind (a garment, a college, a team, a family member), there's a picture to click.
2. **Fewer dead ends.**
   - Every product page has category/theme buttons and **"More like this"**.
   - Sold-out sizes are greyed out and crossed through rather than hidden.
   - Chat answers show up as product cards on the page (Problem 7).
3. **Fewer surprises.** Price and stock show on cards, in "The latest", and in chat, all read live from the database, so shoppers aren't put off by out-of-date details.
4. **Works on phones.**
   - The header collapses to a menu.
   - The hero stacks.
   - Carousels scroll by swipe.
   - The chat panel fits the screen.
