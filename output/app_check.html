2

Chat search that updates the page

Pass, with a note

What was tested

Asking the chat for more items (typed as "how me more crew neck t shirts") to check that the results replace the product grid on the page.

Result

  • Worked: the page switched to a "Handsome Dan's picks" grid with 28 product cards (image, name, price, short description). The chat showed a "28 items shown on the page →" link, and each card still opens its product page.
  • Not quite right: it added too much of the wrong thing. I asked for t-shirts, and these are crewneck sweaters.
Finding, confirmed in the database: all 28 results are from the Crewnecks sub-category (crewneck sweatshirt / raglan crewneck sweatshirt). None of them are t-shirts. The shop does have crew-neck tees, 10 in T-Shirts & Tops (e.g. Yale Bowl, Pierson Logo, Yale Dad), but the agent read "crew neck" as the sweatshirt sub-category.
Suggested fix: add a rule to prompts/prompt.md: when the shopper names a garment (t-shirt, tee, hoodie), that decides the category, and "crew neck" just describes the neckline. Then "crew neck t-shirts" becomes category="T-Shirts & Tops" plus the keyword "crew".
